# 팀원 캐릭터 후보

제작 방식: 내장 이미지 생성 도구

밝은 팀원 소개 페이지와 카드 공개 연출을 위한 검토용 후보입니다.

- [웃음 쿼카](./smile-quokka-v1.png): 사용자가 요청한 웃음 이미지를 표현했습니다. 특정 팀원에게 확정 배정하지 않았습니다.
- [대구 청년 강아지](./daegu-dog-v1.png): 김영광님의 자기소개에 나온 대구 청년, 초긍정, 한 번 물면 놓치지 않는다는 표현을 바탕으로 제안했습니다. 지역 공식 캐릭터가 아닙니다.

## 웃음 쿼카 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original animal mascot candidate for a Korean three-member team introduction and collectible card reveal.
Style/medium: distinctive hand-drawn character illustration with confident slightly irregular dark-ink outlines, simple expressive shapes, restrained flat color with a little colored-pencil grain, soft minimal two-tone shading. A memorable contemporary Korean independent illustrator's character, friendly and youthful, readable as a small card portrait. Natural drawing decisions and appealing expressive silhouette.
Composition/framing: one complete full-body character only, centered, three-quarter view, occupying about 78 percent of a square canvas, every ear, paw and tail fully visible with comfortable margin. No card frame. Plain warm-white background with only a very small soft grounding shadow.
Lighting/mood: bright, open, cheerful daylight feel.
Constraints: original character, no existing IP, no lettering or names, no logo, no watermark, no stats, no additional people or animals, no scenery. Avoid glossy 3D toy render, photoreal fur, neon outlines, dramatic dark background, glowing particles, ornate gold, generic round emoji face, hyper-detailed texture.
Subject: a quokka mascot embodying hearty laughter and warmth. Warm natural brown and cream fur, compact pear-shaped body, round small ears, plump muzzle and expressive cheek creases. Eyes curve nearly closed while laughing, a broad open joyful smile showing a tiny pair of upper teeth, one paw lightly on its belly and the other lifted in a spontaneous friendly greeting. The gently leaning pose should feel like a friend who has just heard a genuinely funny joke. Keep anatomically coherent animal paws and a simple recognizable quokka face, not a human head. No clothing or props.
```

## 대구 청년 강아지 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original animal mascot candidate for a Korean three-member team introduction and collectible card reveal.
Style/medium: distinctive hand-drawn character illustration with confident slightly irregular dark-ink outlines, simple expressive shapes, restrained flat color with a little colored-pencil grain, soft minimal two-tone shading. A memorable contemporary Korean independent illustrator's character, friendly and youthful, readable as a small card portrait. Natural drawing decisions and appealing expressive silhouette.
Composition/framing: one complete full-body character only, centered, three-quarter view, occupying about 78 percent of a square canvas, every ear, paw and tail fully visible with comfortable margin. No card frame. Plain warm-white background with only a very small soft grounding shadow.
Lighting/mood: bright, open, cheerful daylight feel.
Constraints: original character, no existing IP, no lettering or names, no logo, no watermark, no stats, no additional people or animals, no scenery. Avoid glossy 3D toy render, photoreal fur, neon outlines, dramatic dark background, glowing particles, ornate gold, generic round emoji face, hyper-detailed texture.
Subject: an energetic young Jindo-inspired Korean dog mascot, a creative avatar for a team member who describes himself as a positive young person from Daegu and 'once I bite, I never let go'. Convey tenacity, upbeat energy and approachable youthful confidence, never aggression. Warm natural cream and light-tan fur, distinctly pointed upright ears with one ear slightly bent, short expressive canine muzzle, alert bright eyes, and an asymmetrical open friendly grin showing one small harmless canine tooth. Athletic small upright cartoon body, relaxed confident stance with one paw at its hip and the other lifted in a cheerful can-do gesture, curled tail visible. A simple unbranded casual varsity jacket worn open, in a quiet brick-red and cream combination, no letters or patches; this is a young neighborhood friend, not an official city mascot. Use actual animal paws, no human hands. Do not add tourism landmarks or cultural stereotypes.
```
